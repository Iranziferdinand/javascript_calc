📘 How This Calculator Works

1. Input: Click number buttons to build your expression.
2. Operations: Use +, −, ×, ÷ for calculations.
3. Calculate: Press = to evaluate the expression.
4. Clear: C resets everything; ⌫ deletes last digit.
💡 Tip: Uses JavaScript's eval() function to evaluate mathematical expressions in real-time.
